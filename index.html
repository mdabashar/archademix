GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
you might wonder.

Who is Archademix for?+

Students in Years 6–8 who would like support with their subjects, assignments, organisation or study routines. We start with what the student needs help with.

Is this tutoring or study coaching?+

Both. Sessions combine explanations and practice in school subjects with ways to plan assignments, organise revision and build useful study habits.

What should I bring to a session?+

Bring the schoolwork you’d like to focus on, relevant class notes, assignment instructions and any teacher feedback. A specific question or goal is a great starting point.

Can parents make the enquiry?+

Yes. Parents and carers are welcome to discuss the student’s goals and session arrangements. Booking and contact information will be published here once finalised.

Are sessions available online?+

The initial focus is face-to-face tutoring after school. Online sessions may be explored in the future. Please confirm the available format before arranging a session.
